Follow these steps to set up your store and start receiving repair bookings from customers across Ireland.

Quick Overview

获取登录账号
Get Your Login Credentials

Provided directly by the IERepair team

在您正式开始使用之前，请先联系 IERepair 运营团队，我们会为您创建商家账号，并直接告知以下登录信息：

Before you can log in, contact the IERepair team. We will create your merchant account and provide you with the following details directly:

  • 登录邮箱 — 用于登录商家后台的邮箱地址
  • Login Email — the email address used to log in to your merchant portal
  • 初始密码 — 首次登录后系统会要求您立即修改
  • Initial Password — you will be asked to change this immediately on first login
📞 联系方式：📞 Contact: 请通过本页底部的联系方式与 IERepair 团队取得联系。 Reach out via the contact details at the bottom of this page.
2
首次登录 & 修改密码
First Login & Change Password
安全第一，临时密码必须立即更换
Your temporary password must be changed immediately

打开商家后台登录页：

Open the merchant login page:

https://ierepair-dfd7b2qpra-ew.a.run.app/merchant/login
  • 输入邮件中的登录邮箱和临时密码，点击「登录」
  • Enter your email and the temporary password from the email, then click Log In
  • 系统将自动跳转至「修改密码」页面（首次登录强制执行）
  • You will be automatically redirected to the Change Password page (required on first login)
  • 输入新密码（建议至少 8 位，包含字母和数字）并确认
  • Enter a new password (at least 8 characters, mixing letters and numbers is recommended) and confirm
  • 修改成功后，系统自动跳转至商家控制台首页（Dashboard）
  • Once changed, you will be taken to your merchant Dashboard
💡 提示：💡 Tip: 请妥善保管您的新密码，不要与他人分享。如忘记密码，请联系 IERepair 运营团队重置。 Keep your new password safe. If you forget it, contact the IERepair team to reset it.
3
完善门店资料
Complete Your Store Profile
消费者搜索时看到的就是这些信息
This is what customers see when they find your store

进入 Settings（门店设置） 页面，填写门店的基本信息：

Go to Settings and fill in your store details:

https://ierepair-dfd7b2qpra-ew.a.run.app/merchant/settings
填写项目 Field 是否必填 Required?
门店名称 Store Name 必填Yes
联系电话 Phone Number 必填Yes
门店地址 Address 必填Yes
Eircode（爱尔兰邮编） Eircode 必填，用于地图搜索定位Yes — used for map search
门店简介 Description 建议填写，吸引更多客户Recommended
营业时间 Opening Hours 必填，每天独立设置Yes — set separately for each day
门店照片 Store Photos 建议上传，提高信任度Recommended
⚠️ 重要：⚠️ Important: Eircode 决定了您的门店在消费者按地区搜索时能否出现。请务必填写正确，格式如：D01 W2X2 Your Eircode determines whether your store appears in location-based searches. Enter it correctly, e.g. D01 W2X2

设置营业时间：在设置页面下方的营业时间表中，为每天分别选择开始时间和结束时间。休息日请勾选"关闭"。填写完毕后点击保存。

Setting opening hours: In the Hours section of Settings, set opening and closing times for each day of the week. Toggle a day as "Closed" if you're not open. Click Save when done.

4
添加维修服务
Add Your Repair Services
告诉消费者你能修什么、收多少钱
Tell customers what you repair and at what price

进入 Services（维修服务） 页面：

Go to the Services page:

https://ierepair-dfd7b2qpra-ew.a.run.app/merchant/services

添加服务分为 4 步：

Adding a service takes 4 steps:

1
选择设备类别
Choose device category
如：iPhone、Samsung、iPad 等
e.g. iPhone, Samsung, iPad
2
选择品牌和具体型号
Select brand and model
如：Apple → iPhone 15 Pro
e.g. Apple → iPhone 15 Pro
3
选择维修类型并填写您的价格
Choose repair type and set your price
如：屏幕更换 €89，电池更换 €49
e.g. Screen Replacement €89, Battery €49
4
点击「添加」，服务即时生效
Click Add — the service goes live immediately
消费者搜索时即可看到您的报价
Customers can see your price in search results right away
💡 提示：💡 Tip: 建议添加您最擅长的 5–10 种常见维修服务，并确保价格有竞争力。之后随时可以在服务列表中开启或关闭某项服务。 We recommend adding 5–10 common repairs you specialise in with competitive prices. You can enable or disable any service at any time from the services list.
5
接单与管理预约
Managing Bookings
消费者下单后在此处处理
Handle customer repair bookings here

进入 Bookings（预约管理） 页面：

Go to the Bookings page:

https://ierepair-dfd7b2qpra-ew.a.run.app/merchant/bookings

预约订单共有以下几个状态：

Booking statuses explained:

待确认 Pending Pending Confirmation 已确认 Confirmed Confirmed 维修中 In Progress In Progress 已完成 Completed Completed 已取消 Cancelled Cancelled
  • 接受预约：点击订单上的「接受」按钮，状态变为「已确认」，消费者会看到确认通知
  • Accept: Click the Accept button on the booking. Status changes to Confirmed and the customer is notified
  • 拒绝预约：点击「拒绝」按钮，状态变为「已取消」
  • Reject: Click Reject to cancel the booking
  • 完成维修：维修完成后，点击「标记完成」，状态变为「已完成」
  • Complete: Once the repair is done, click Mark as Complete
⚠️ 建议：⚠️ Recommendation: 请在消费者下单后 24 小时内完成确认或拒绝，避免影响消费者体验和平台评分。 Please confirm or reject bookings within 24 hours to maintain a good rating and customer experience.
6
扫码签到（可选）
QR Check-In (Optional)
消费者到店时扫描其手机上的二维码
Scan the customer's QR code when they arrive

消费者在「我的预约」页面可生成一个签到二维码。当其到店时，商家可进入「Check-in」页面扫描二维码完成签到，免去人工核验订单号的麻烦。

Customers can show a QR code from their booking history. Open the Check-in page on your device and scan their QR code to instantly verify and check in the booking — no need to look up order numbers manually.

https://ierepair-dfd7b2qpra-ew.a.run.app/merchant/checkin
💡 提示：💡 Tip: 建议在店内设置一台平板或电脑常开 Check-in 页面，方便接待到店客户。 Consider keeping the Check-in page open on a tablet or PC at your counter for quick customer reception.
